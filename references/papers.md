# Academic Paper References

## Core Pipeline Papers

### Stage 1 — SciExplorer
- **Nägele, M. & Marquardt, F.** (2025). "Agentic Exploration of Physics Models." *Physical Review X*. [arXiv:2509.24978v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24978)
  - LLM-driven autonomous discovery of equations of motion, Hamiltonians, and physical laws through numerical experimentation.
  - Core innovation: observe → reason → experiment → analyze → repeat agent loop.

### Stage 2 — SimpleTES
- **SimpleTES** (2025). "Structured Test-time Evaluation-driven Scaling for AI Discovery." [arXiv:2604.19341](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19341)
  - Systematic (C,L,K,Φ) evaluation-budget scaling framework.
  - rpucg DAG-aware selector with γ-decay for proof candidate ranking.

### Stage 3 — Physics Proof Engine
- **Deng-Hani** (2026). "Kinetic Theory of Phase Transitions in Strongly Coupled Gauge Theories." *(integration reference)*
  - FRG flow, Wetterich equation, NJL gap bridge formalism.
- **Kibble, T.W.B.** (1976). "Topology of cosmic domains and strings." *J. Phys. A: Math. Gen.*, 9(8), 1387.
  - Kibble-Zurek mechanism — foundational reference for topological defect proofs.
- **Zurek, W.H.** (1985). "Cosmological experiments in superfluid helium?" *Nature*, 317, 505.
  - KZ mechanism in condensed matter systems.

### Stage 4 — AI-Scientist V2
- **Sakana AI** (2025). "AI Scientist-v2: Workshop-Level Automated Scientific Discovery." [arXiv:2504.08066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08066)
